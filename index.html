About Me

I'm Icaro, an independent game developer with professional experience in Unity since 2021. I specialize in gameplay programming, 2D game development, and creating engaging interactive experiences, combining technical skills with creativity to bring game concepts to life. Throughout my career, I've developed and published games on platforms such as CrazyGames and GamePix, as well as collaborated with international clients on game development projects. My experience covers a wide range of tasks, including gameplay mechanics, user interface implementation, animations, particle effects, level design, bug fixing, and performance optimization. I enjoy working on projects from initial concepts to finished products, focusing on creating smooth gameplay, intuitive mechanics, and enjoyable player experiences. I'm also comfortable adapting to different project requirements, working independently, solving technical challenges, and communicating with clients to ensure their expectations are met. I have C2-level English proficiency, certified by Pearson Versant, and regularly communicate with international clients and professionals in English. This allows me to collaborate effectively with people from different backgrounds and contribute confidently to international development teams.
